# Third-Party Assets

The assets below are used as supporting weapon visuals. They are not counted as
custom models created by the OVERRIDE team.

| Asset | Publisher | Unity Asset Store product | Project use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sci-Fi Gun Light | Hs HYPERION studio | [Product 87916](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/guns/sci-fi-gun-light-87916) | Energy pistol visual |
| Sci Fi Gun | MASH Virtual | [Product 162872](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/guns/sci-fi-gun-162872) | EMP heavy-gun visual |

Both packages were obtained through the Unity Asset Store and remain subject to
the Unity Asset Store license. Do not redistribute their source assets outside
the licensed project/team.

Project-owned URP materials and wrapper prefabs are stored under
`Assets/_Project/`. The custom pulse rifle remains an original team asset.
